### Remaining bounded high-impact work

1. **Expressive actor and discovery reactions.** Bris and the fisherman largely keep the same pose; the emotional turn is delivered mostly by text. Add authored pet/head/ear attention, clear rod/body loading, landing/release motion, and a distinct uneasy reaction to abnormal fish. Judge it in motion, not by counting effects in source.
2. **Living surface/environment.** The upper lake's large reflection field reads mainly as baked painting. Gentle localized reflection refraction, mist drift, and shoreline/wake integration are the largest remaining scene-craft improvement. Keep motion subtle and spatially coherent; avoid wobbling shore geometry. Sparse current delivery prevents evaluating smoothness honestly.
3. **Time-of-day coherence.** If all four modes are a promised feature, day needs a genuinely daylight sky/light treatment rather than sunset tint. At minimum adapt idle copy and describe dawn/day as variants. This is polish/content scope, not a newly broken control after the dawn fix.

No remaining blocking overlap was observed in the tested desktop and tall portrait layouts after fixes. Shorter phone heights remain untested by this critic. The disabled setup-depth slider still consumes space during fights; deemphasizing it would improve focus, but it is a smaller issue than the three priorities above.
