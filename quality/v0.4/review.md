# Independent Gauntlet review — v0.4

Read-only critic living_waters_critic inspected six actual browser screenshots and both corrected desktop captures. Scale: 10 = top modern art-directed game polish.

First pass: 8.0/10. Biomes 8.8, boats 7.5, mobile visibility 8.3, schools/underwater 7.9, diary/shop 8.6, material consistency 7.2. Two blockers: obvious three-row water repetition and a floating rectangular lantern.

Final pass: **8.2/10**, strict >8 still-image gate passed. Continuous water sampling connects reflections to landscape; framed lantern is mounted on the gunwale. Remaining non-blocking issues: water is softer/stretched compared to the sharp boat and landscape, schools could vary further in size/spacing, mixed location names remain. This is not evidence for 60fps, continuous animation, audio quality or game balance.
